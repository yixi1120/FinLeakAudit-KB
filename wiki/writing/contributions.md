# Contributions (Draft)

## Draft v3 (Current)

1. **We identify and quantify evidence-level temporal leakage** in Agentic RAG financial forecasting, showing that future evidence can materially alter forecast direction and confidence.

2. **We propose FinLeakAudit**, an audit protocol consisting of temporal availability checking, claim-evidence attribution, support/contradiction verification, evidence ablation, and leakage-driven forecast revision.

3. **We introduce an evaluation protocol** for leakage-aware financial forecasting, measuring prediction performance, evidence leakage rate, rationale faithfulness, revision effectiveness, and computational cost.
